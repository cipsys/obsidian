```
Present a report in no more than 2 pages for an executive audience on the impact of recent government shutdown on Veterans. Provide detailed analysis on impacted programs, quality of care and have a detailed set of identified opportunities to mitigate these impacts as much as possible.
```

```
Present a report in no more than 2 pages for an executive audience on how AI is reshaping workforce skill requirements, career paths, and the lifecycle of professional expertise. The report should evaluate which skills are becoming obsolete, which are increasing in demand, and which reskilling approaches are proving effective in preparing workers for AI-augmented roles.

The research should address:
- Shifts from role-based skills to capability- and task-based skill models    
- The changing half-life of technical, analytical, and professional skills
- Effectiveness of enterprise-led, government-led, and individual-driven reskilling initiatives
- Barriers to successful reskilling at scale (time, incentives, access, culture)
- Implications for talent development, education systems, and workforce mobility
```
