### Segment 1: FBI Background Investigation App (7 min)

**Demo:** Show the FBI app — graph analysis, automated SF-86 processing, risk scoring, AI chat with case-specific RAG.

**Strategic translation:**

>_"This isn't just a demo — it's our **proof of the land-and-expand model**. We deployed FedGenius as a GenAI platform. Then,  **without touching core**, a small team built a mission-critical investigation system on top of it. That's the pattern we replicate."_ 

**Growth narrative:**

- **The play:** Every federal agency has 3-5 manual, document-heavy workflows that are begging for this treatment (adjudications, FOIA processing, grant reviews, compliance audits, case management)
- **The economics:** The FBI app is a _microapp_ — isolated, independent, built on FedGenius infrastructure. One platform sale becomes 3-5 custom app engagements per agency
- **The moat:** Once an agency has FedGenius + 2-3 custom apps, they're not ripping it out. The switching cost isn't the chat — it's the mission apps built on top
- **Pipeline example:** "If we go to DHS, DOJ, or VA with this pattern — here's your AI platform, and here's what a domain-specific app looks like on day 60 — that's a fundamentally different conversation than selling a chatbot"

>**One-liner to land:** _"Custom apps are where the platform becomes indispensable and where the multi-year revenue lives."_

### Segment 2: Live Agent Creation —Requirements & Product Backlog (7 min)

**Demo:** Build an agent live that researches a topic online and emails the findings. Show scheduling, tool selection, execution tracking.

**Strategic translation:**

> _"What I'm about to show you is the shift from AI-as-assistant to AI-as-worker. This is what agencies will pay for in 2027."_

**Growth narrative:**

- **The play:** Agents solve the "so what?" problem. Executives get that chat is useful, but agents deliver _measurable output_ — a report generated, a compliance check completed, an alert sent
- **The economics:** Agents are recurring workloads. A scheduled agent running daily compliance checks is ongoing O&M revenue, not a one-time implementation
- **The differentiator vs. competition:** ChatGPT and Copilot can chat. They can't schedule an autonomous agent that runs at 6am, queries 3 data sources, and emails a summary to a program manager — inside an agency's security boundary
- **The buyer conversation:** "We're not selling AI features. We're selling headcount reduction on repetitive analytical work. An agent that runs nightly costs a fraction of an FTE doing the same manual pull"

**One-liner to land:** _"Agents turn AI from a tool people use into a system that does work. That's the step change that justifies enterprise budgets."_
